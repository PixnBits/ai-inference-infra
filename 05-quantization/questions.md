# Socratic prompts — Stage 5

## Prediction
1. For a 7B model, what VRAM reduction and latency improvement do you expect from INT4 versus FP16?
2. Do you expect the quality drop to be larger on short or long generations?

## Measurement
3. What were the actual latency, VRAM, and quality numbers? Cite the method.
4. Did any tokens or sequences show visibly worse quality? How did you detect it?

## Reflection
5. When is the quality drop acceptable for your use case?
6. What is the single most important calibration detail you would change next, and how would you measure the effect?
